Pairwise Writing Task

Creating a document with two AI assistants (side-by-side)

In this task, you will collaborate with two AI assistants at the same time on a short document, comparing their responses side-by-side. The task is broken into four sequential steps: a writing-topic setup, a brief training session so you can get familiar with the interface, and then the real session on the same topic.

  1. Step 1 — Choose a writing topic. You will be assigned a document type (blog post, creative writing, or personal statement) and choose an intent (the topic you want to write about) from a list of possible writing topics.
  2. Step 2 — Pre-writing. Before talking to the AIs, you will briefly jot down your thoughts on a few preparation questions. These notes are just for you — the AIs do not see them.
  3. Step 3 — Training session (1 exchange). Before the real session begins, you'll do a short 1-exchange warm-up with the side-by-side interface so the format is familiar. You'll send one message, compare the two replies, and pick a preference with a brief rationale. After you submit your preference, the page will refresh automatically.
  4. Step 4 — Real session (minimum 5 exchanges). On the same writing topic, you'll have a minimum 5-exchange conversation with the two AI assistants. At each exchange you will see replies from two different AI models side-by-side (labeled "Model A" and "Model B"). After each pair of replies, you pick the one you prefer and briefly say why — both AIs then pick up from that chosen reply for the next message.
Requirements:
  • The training session is 1 exchange; the real session is minimum 5 exchanges.
  • After every exchange, you'll pick "A is better" / "Tie" / "B is better" with a brief one-sentence rationale.
  • The final document (from the real session) should be at least 200 words.
  • The page will refresh automatically between training and the real session — please do not close the tab.
Important — the AIs do not know your intent in advance. Both AI assistants start blind: they don't see your document type, your chosen intent, or your pre-writing notes. You must tell them what you want to write through your own messages. Your pre-writing notes are on your screen as a reference for you — they are not sent to the AIs.
Tips:
  • Open the conversation by telling the AIs, in your own words, what you want to write.
  • Make a genuine effort to engage with the AIs thoughtfully.
  • Please do not paste all your pre-writing notes at once — please use your own words.
  • Don't worry if the discussion takes a different direction than what you initially planned in notes.
  • Note that the AI model may take a few seconds to return a response — don't worry, this is normal.
  • If you see content that seems inappropriate, use the "Report" button on the assistant's response.

A detailed guide for what to look for when comparing replies will be shown at the top of the task page — you don't need to memorize it now.
