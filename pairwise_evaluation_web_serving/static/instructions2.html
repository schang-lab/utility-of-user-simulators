Important Information

Before you begin, please read these guidelines carefully. They apply throughout the study.

Important: Please do not enter any personally identifiable or sensitive information (such as your name, address, or health details) in your messages at any point during this study.
Please complete the task on your own. Do not use outside sources such as Google, Wikipedia, or other AI tools (like ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini) to help with your writing or messages. We want to understand how you collaborate with the AI in this study directly.
Please complete in a single uninterrupted run. To ensure your responses are properly recorded and to accurately track the time and effort you spend on the task, do not refresh the page, go to the previous page, close the browser tab, or take a long break.

Quality Check

We review all submissions for quality before approving compensation. To help us get the most out of this study — and to make sure your submission is approved — please keep the following in mind:

  • Time: Your activity timestamps are recorded. Submissions that finish unusually quickly or include long gaps will be reviewed.
  • Message quality: Messages that are very short, off-topic, or appear copied from external sources will be flagged. Please engage genuinely with the writing task in your own words.
  • Preference rationales: Generic rationales like "this one is better" will be flagged. Please mention something specific about why you preferred one response over the other.
  • External tools: Using outside sources (Google, Wikipedia, other AI tools) is not permitted and will be flagged.
